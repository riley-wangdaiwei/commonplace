# Guidance for producing a Campbell evidence and gap map (White et al., 2020)

Part of [[hub-evidence-gap]].

## Thesis

Guidance for producing a Campbell evidence and gap map.

## Quotes
- "EGMs are a systematic evidence synthesis product which display the available evidence relevant to a specific research question. The scope of a map is generally broader than that of a systematic review. We distinguish between EGMs which contain primary studies and reviews, mega-maps which include reviews and other maps, and maps of maps which contain only other maps."

## Notes

- Make categories comprehensive and mutually exclusive
- Produce a dictionary of terms
- Prefer a few large gaps to many tiny categories. 
	- This is mainly considering for users' navigation - seeing the whole picture is the point of an EGM. But I think the better way is to use a more continuous axis and a heat map that is contour/continuous, so that users 1) get the whole picture because continuous functions make the whole picture meaningful and 2) can zoom in to specific parts at the same time.
	- different levels of maps (eg. EGMs, Megamaps, MofMs that it mentioned) could be added to provide the "zoom-in effect".
- Consult the stakeholders. 
	- Which gaps matter in practice, and what makes the evidence difficult to collect or use? --> which gaps should be verified (included)
