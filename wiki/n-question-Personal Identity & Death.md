## Question
If someone thinks she wants to be another person, (with another identity), is that similar to something like suicide? Is there a link between someone wanting to commit a suicide and someone wanting to go back to her childhood?

## Why it matters
* Investigating the psychological and philosophical boundaries of personal identity, analyzing whether the desire to erase or reset the self (becoming another person or regressing to childhood) shares structural motives with death or identity extinction.

## Next
* Read Galen Strawson's "Against Narrativity" to examine non-narrative selfhood (Episodism) and its implications for identity continuity under extreme pressure.
