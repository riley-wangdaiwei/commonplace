# Q25: Is my bad work ethic linked to "the stickiness" of my behavior? How to solve it?

## My Symptoms

1. 很难进入工作，一天内要么什么都不做，要么只做一件事。
2. 一旦开始了一件事，就容易停不下来，而其它事则更难开始。
3. 有的事，刚布置完，不开始做，就能拖很久不做，直到来不及。

## Solutions

### Implementation intention

- Definition (Webb & Sheeran, 2007): implementation intentions are plans that specify when, where, and how of goal striving in advance.
- "Implementation intention forges an association between that cue and a response that is instrumental for obtaining one's goal."
- How it works: implementation intention effects were mediated by the accessibility of the specified cue and by the strength of cue-response links. That is, a mental link is formed between the if component and the then component of the plan.

### Cues

- Altman & Trafton (2004): when you return, relevant cues — such as the task interface or information about your previous work — help you retrieve the task's context from memory, which leads to faster resumption.
