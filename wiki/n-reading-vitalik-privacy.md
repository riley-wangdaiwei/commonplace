# Why I support Privacy

## Notes

- 2 optimistic assumptions behind 'privacy is dead': (1) top-level global politics well-intentioned enough that vertical privacy is unneeded; (2) culture keeps evolving so horizontal privacy is unneeded
- Privacy = not having to weigh others' behaviors much before making decisions (public games); side games
