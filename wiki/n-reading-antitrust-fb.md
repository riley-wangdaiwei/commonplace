# Antitrust Economics at a time of upheaval: Chp14 The German Facebook Case

## Thesis

Facebook's supra-competitive data collection = abuse of dominance, equivalent to excessive pricing in a zero-price market

## Notes

- EU vs US: lack of post-Chicago analysis in EU cases; in Europe competition law is lawyers' realm
- Dominance: attention market - companies compete for user attention; active users determine dominance; user becomes the product
- Abuse / as-if competition: would yield social networks not requiring WhatsApp+Instagram data linkage
- Facebook zero-cost; supra-competitive data collection and usage = excessive pricing, enforceable only via dominant position
- Benchmarks for excess: price difference? profit margins? FCO: abuse = not respecting rules protecting consumers in unbalanced bargaining position
- super-profiles = quality deterioration = price increase in zero-price market
- harm starts when one market side imposes rules on the other ('take-it-or-leave-it'); FCO: consumers empowered vs dominant firms is prerequisite for competitive process
- competitors at disadvantage if they respected GDPR
- FCO ruling: bundling social network + massive personalized data collection; Hayekian: markets are decentralized info systems, competition needs meaningful options; giant data reservoir = entry barrier; consumers pay via data exploitation harm
- pros for users: efficiency. cons: data leaking; data collection may make users change behavior to please others/public bodies
