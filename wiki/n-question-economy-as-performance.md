# Is earning money actually the value you generate, or is it just the result of a game you participate in?

## How this question is formed

- Being a buyer and a seller feels like a performance, not something natural.
- Money feels like a prop (道具) in a game called economics.
- People spend money because they have money; if they didn't, they'd only buy necessary goods, not interesting things.
- You depend on others to produce interesting goods for you, and you pay them money — basically to keep playing the game.
- Becoming an adult, becoming a businessman, feels magical: how do you earn money? Somehow you just earn it.
- Earning money doesn't feel like the value you generated — it feels like the result of a game you participated in.
- To earn money you have to perform: make people believe you have value — as if you already have money — in order to earn money.
- The other side of the same performance: people feel they have to spend money because they have it, so they give it to you.
- You can end up with a product that has no use whatsoever — and the exchange still happens through the performance.
